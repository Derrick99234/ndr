## ABOUT NIGHT OF DIVINE REVERSAL (NDR)

The Night of Divine Reversal (NDR), convened by Prophet Isaiah Macwealth, is a special prophetic vigil that serves as the bi-monthly or quarterly Grand Finale of the 3 Days of Remembrance, hosted at the Ark of Light for all Nations, the international headquarters of Gospel Pillars Intl. Church.

The Night of Divine Reversal is designed for supernatural turnaround and decisive spiritual intervention. It features praise, prophecy and the manifestation of the power of God. The meeting is also characterized by intimate spiritual elevation, supernatural encounters, deep revelation of God's Word, accompanied by divine power and the manifestation of the Holy Spirit.

### NDR Focuses On:
* Reversing negative patterns and long-standing issues
* Overturning evil decrees and limitations
* Restoring lost opportunities, time, and virtue
* Enforcing divine justice and destiny realignment
* Deeper spiritual intensity, warfare, and concentrated prophetic action.

## PROGRAMME SCHEDULE

The Night of Divine Reversal (NDR) is preceded by a week-long set of activities which includes:

* **Day 1 – 3 (Mon. to Wed.): Victory and Faith Classes**  
  This is an arm of the OneSound Bible Institute (OBI), established as an extension of the teaching ministry of the Prophet. It is committed to simplifying and communicating biblical knowledge in a clear and practical manner, equipping and empowering God's people for spiritual growth, personal development, and purposeful living. The initiative is anchored on the Word of the Lord to His servant, Prophet Isaiah Macwealth: *"Teaching the people to grow is the fastest way to eradicate their challenges."*  
  Interested participants are to register online via: [https://onesoundbibleinstitute.org/vfc](https://onesoundbibleinstitute.org/vfc)

* **Day 3 (Wed.): All Nations Remembrance & Revival Service**  
  This is a special intercessory session for revival and divine remembrance for nations of the world. The service is broadcast LIVE on Facebook and YouTube and several other online and TV platforms.

* **Day 4 (Thurs.): Victory and Faith Remembrance Prayer Line**  
  This is a special one-on-one ministration session with the man of God, Prophet Isaiah Macwealth, which also features prayer sessions, administration of Sweet Water, deliverance, healing miracles and demonstration of the power of God to break every yoke and affliction of the devil. You can register to be on the Victory and Faith Remembrance line via: [https://onesoundbibleinstitute.org/vfc](https://onesoundbibleinstitute.org/vfc)

* **Day 5 (Friday): Night of Divine Reversal**  
  The week-long event culminates in the Night of Divine Reversal, a service of deep fellowship with the Holy Spirit, where spiritual strength is renewed, burdens are lifted, and destinies are aligned according to God's perfect will. It is a sacred atmosphere where hearts are revived, faith is stirred, and lives are transformed by the tangible presence of God.

*Overall, it is a week of ministrations, faith classes, intercession, deliverance, administration of sweet water and the night of praise, prayer and power, which holds at the Ark of Light for all Nations.*
